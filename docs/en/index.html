04 / BEFORE YOU DEPLOY

Questions? Start here.

Free backend, licensing, migration, backups, and Excel—all the information you need in one place.

?
01

Why is the backend free? How is the product funded?

The self-hosted backend is free infrastructure, with no server subscription per employee. The official iOS client funds the product through a one-time per-device access purchase. Two months of full access start with the first successful company binding. After trial expiry, new sync pauses without purchase; locally saved history remains available behind system authentication.

02

What does free self-hosting include?

OpsHome Payslip provides a free self-hosted community edition. Companies can deploy official Docker images under the product license and use the official iOS client. Free deployment does not grant permission to modify or redistribute server source.

03

After a server failure or migration, must employees pair again?

Restore an encrypted snapshot containing the device registry and retain the original company HTTPS address and matching trust settings. Existing devices in that snapshot can continue; the companion app recovers rotated sessions with signed device proof. Changes after the snapshot, including new bindings and revocations, are not in the older snapshot. Keep current backups and protect the passphrase.

04

Can we import 20–30 columns with changing monthly headings?

Yes. Headers and row counts follow the actual file. Manually map employee number, name and net pay; other columns are treated as amounts or text, with independent totals for every amount column. Cross-period reports align identical column names, so companies should standardize equivalent fields. Upload resource protections and Excel capacity limits still apply.
