Privacy Policy

Updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers the official OpsHome Payslip iOS client and this product website. Also consult your employer’s privacy notice for the specific arrangements of its self-hosted server.

1. Our role and your employer’s role

OpsHome provides the payslip client and self-hosted server software. Your employer configures and operates its payroll server and determines the purposes, access permissions and retention periods for employee profiles, payslips, approvals, acknowledgements and disputes. Contact your HR, finance or system administrator first to access, correct or request deletion of payroll records.

2. Information used to connect to your employer

Pairing and use require processing company connection settings, employee identity information (such as employee number, name, department and position), device binding information, payslips, viewing or confirmation status, and disputes and replies you choose to submit. Fields depend on employer settings and the features you use. This business data passes between the client and the connected employer server; payroll content is not uploaded as part of official license verification.

3. Encrypted local cache and unlocking

Synced payslips are cached in encrypted form on the device for offline access. Device keys and system security mechanisms protect binding and access. iOS handles biometric authentication; the App does not read or store Face ID facial information, Touch ID fingerprint templates or your device passcode. Uninstalling the App does not delete employer server records. Clearing local data or unbinding a device does not delete data retained by your employer.

4. Device security and Apple services

Depending on the version and server configuration, device binding and request verification may involve a device public key, binding identifiers, request signatures, timestamps and Apple App Attest verification data. Purchases and restoration use Apple services; transaction credentials and necessary device binding information may be used for official licensing and employer server verification. We do not obtain full payment card details through in-app purchases, and payroll amounts or payslip details are not required to be sent to Apple for license verification. Apple processes its service data under its own privacy policy.

5. Permissions, network access and logs

QR pairing uses the camera with your permission. Biometrics and notifications are used according to the available features and system permissions. The App connects to your selected employer server and relevant Apple or official verification services. Employer servers, reverse proxies and website hosting services may generate necessary logs such as IP addresses, access times, request results and security events. Retention is determined by the respective operator.

6. Website and demonstration data

Employees, amounts and pairing interactions shown on this website are fictional demonstrations. Demonstrations run locally in your browser and are not used to submit real payroll data. These pages do not embed third-party advertising, analytics or tracking scripts. The hosting provider may still process necessary access logs. External links, including App Store and Docker Hub, are subject to those services’ privacy practices.

7. Sharing, retention and security limits

Payroll processing takes place through your connected employer and its selected infrastructure. Apple and relevant verification services process information needed for purchases, licensing or security checks. This policy does not prescribe a single deployment location or retention period for employer logs and backups. Your employer should explain its providers, storage regions and retention arrangements. Encryption, device binding and screen masking reduce exposure but cannot prevent external-camera photography or every device compromise. You and your recipients are responsible for protecting exported or shared files.

8. Your choices and how to contact us

Manage granted permissions in iOS Settings and manage device bindings and local data using the available App features. Contact your employer administrator about payroll records, acknowledgements, disputes or employee accounts. For client privacy or security questions, contact the developer through the “App Support” link on this App’s App Store page. Do not send full payslips, passwords, pairing codes or private keys. Changes to this policy will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
