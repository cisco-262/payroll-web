This policy explains the scope of OpsHome Payslip services, responsibility for data management, and software use requirements.

1. What we provide

We provide validated, officially released self-hosted server images, an official iOS App with security protections, and subsequent software updates. Users deploy and manage their own company servers.

2. Your data is managed by you

Users manage business data, including employee profiles, payslips, acknowledgements and disputes, on their own servers and devices. We do not receive, host or manage this business data, and cannot access your self-hosted server or its data through the product’s ordinary services.

3. Data protection and your responsibilities

The product uses encryption and access protections to help protect data, but no software can guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for their server environment, access permissions, device security, backups, restoration and retention arrangements, and for safeguarding passwords, keys and backup files. Do not send us sensitive data or access credentials.

4. Scope of our services

We provide software and updates. We do not operate self-hosted servers on your behalf, retain or restore business data for you, or verify payroll accuracy. Employees with payroll questions or requests to access, correct or delete employer-held data should contact their employer. Software updates do not replace your backup and security responsibilities.

5. Software use requirements

Use of the server software and official iOS App must comply with applicable law, security requirements and software license terms. Do not use the product for unlawful activities, unauthorized access to others’ systems or data, or interference with security protections. Except where expressly permitted by applicable law, unauthorized decompilation, disassembly, reverse engineering, cracking, tampering, circumvention of license verification, and distribution of modified versions are prohibited.

6. Other services and policy updates

The statements about business data in this policy do not mean that Apple purchase and verification services or website hosting services process no information necessary for their operation. Their respective privacy practices apply to that processing. For product privacy or use questions, contact the developer through the “App Support” link on the App’s App Store page. Changes to this policy will be posted here.
